What happens on the line

A filling machine doses a precise amount of liquid or powder into vials, ampoules, or syringes.

This process takes place inside a cleanroom, an enclosed space designed to keep the environment under strict control.

Why precision matters

Accurate dosing helps protect the patient, since every unit must contain the intended amount of product.

It also helps avoid losses at the batch level, which can occur when filling is inconsistent.
