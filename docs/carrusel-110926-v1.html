What It Means

Filling inside a cleanroom

Sterile filling is the process of dosing liquid or powder into vials, ampoules, or syringes.

This happens inside a controlled cleanroom, designed to keep the environment free of contaminants during production.

Why It Matters

Precision protects the patient

Accurate dosing in every vial or syringe helps protect the patient receiving the final product.

It also helps avoid under- or over-filling, which can lead to the loss of an entire production batch.
