What is sterile filling?

Filling machines dose liquid or powder into vials, ampoules, or syringes inside a clean room — a controlled environment designed to minimize contamination.

Each dose must be measured with precision before the container is sealed.

03 / 04

Why precision matters

• Accurate filling helps protect the patient receiving the dose.
• Consistent dosing helps avoid rejecting or losing entire production batches.
• Reliable equipment behavior supports compliance with applicable regulations.
