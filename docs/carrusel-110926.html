What is sterile filling?

Dosing inside a clean room

A filling line doses liquid or powder into vials, ampoules, or syringes inside a controlled clean room environment.

This controlled setting helps keep the product free from contamination throughout the process.

2 / 4

Why it matters on the floor

Precision protects, and prevents loss

Accurate dosing helps protect the patient receiving the final product.

It also helps avoid variations that could lead to losing an entire production lot.
