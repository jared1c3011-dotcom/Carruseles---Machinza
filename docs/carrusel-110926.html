What It Means

Dosing inside a controlled environment

A filling line doses liquid or powder product into vials, ampoules, or syringes.

This happens inside a clean room, designed to keep the product free from contamination during sterile filling.

Why It Matters

Small variations, big consequences

Fill accuracy directly affects patient safety — every vial or syringe must contain the intended dose.

Inconsistent dosing can lead to rejected units and can put an entire production lot at risk.
