What it means

Dosing inside a controlled cleanroom

A filling line doses liquid or powder product into vials, ampoules or syringes.

This process happens inside a cleanroom, an environment designed to keep the product free of contamination.

Why it matters

Precision is not optional

Accurate filling helps protect the patient by keeping each dose consistent and within specification.

It can also help avoid losing an entire production batch due to filling errors.
