What it means

Inside the clean room

A filling machine doses liquid or powder into vials, ampoules, or syringes with controlled accuracy.

This process takes place inside a clean room, an environment designed to limit contamination during production.

Why it matters

Precision protects, imprecision costs

Accurate dosing helps protect the patient who ultimately receives the product.

It also helps avoid losses that can affect an entire production batch.
