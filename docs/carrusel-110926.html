What Is It

Sterile Filling, Defined

Sterile filling is the process of dosing a liquid or powder product into its final container — vial, ampoule, or syringe — inside a controlled environment free of microbial contamination.

Every drop matters. One contaminated unit can compromise an entire production batch.

Inside the Cleanroom

Filling happens inside an ISO-classified cleanroom, where air filtration, pressure control, and strict gowning protocols keep particle and microbial counts near zero.
