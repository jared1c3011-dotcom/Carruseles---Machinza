What Is It
Sterile Filling & Packaging

A controlled process where liquid or powder drugs are dosed into vials, ampoules or pre-filled syringes inside a certified cleanroom — free of microbial contamination.

How It Works
Automated Dosing Process
①PLC-controlled pumps measure exact volume per container, cycle after cycle.
②Laminar airflow keeps particles and microorganisms away from the open container.
③Inline sensors verify fill level in real time before capping.
Why It Matters
Precision Protects Patients

A fraction of a milliliter off-target can mean an incorrect dose — or an entire rejected batch. Accurate filling reduces waste, ensures dosage safety and keeps production compliant.
