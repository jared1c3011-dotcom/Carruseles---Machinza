3 CRITICAL FILLING LINE ERRORS
That trigger batch rejection in sterile pharmaceutical production — and how to prevent them.
Swipe to see the risks →
Error 01 / 03
Miscalibrated Filling Head
Consequence
Volume deviation beyond tolerance limits leads to underfilled or overfilled units — a direct cause of batch rejection.
Prevention
Automated calibration routines and real-time dosing sensors with PLC feedback control.
Error 02 / 03
Product Viscosity Fluctuations
Consequence
Changes in viscosity cause inconsistent fill volumes, foaming, and contamination risk during dosing.
Prevention
Inline viscosity sensors linked to adaptive flow control compensate automatically in real time.
Error 03 / 03
Poor Sync: Filling, Capping & Conveying
Consequence
Timing mismatches cause jams, spillage, and mechanical stress across the line — halting production.
Prevention
Fully integrated PLC control synchronizing every stage of the filling line in real time.
