Sterile Filling Lines:
3 Mistakes That Cause Batch Rejection

Swipe through to see what goes wrong on the line — and how to prevent it.

02 / 04
Mistake 01

Miscalibrated Filling Head

A filling head that drifts out of calibration delivers inconsistent doses — some vials underfilled, others overfilled.

Consequence: Out-of-spec fill volumes trigger automatic batch rejection during QA checks.
03 / 04
Mistakes 02 & 03

Viscosity Shifts & Poor Line Sync

Temperature-driven viscosity changes throw off dosing accuracy, while unsynced filling, capping and conveying create jams.

Consequence: Contamination risk, vial breakage and stalled lines — all leading to lot rejection.
04 / 04
The Fix

Control & Automation

  • PLC-based real-time monitoring keeps the filling head calibrated automatically.
  • Servo-driven dosing compensates for viscosity changes on the fly.
  • Synchronized conveyor logic aligns filling, capping and transport.
