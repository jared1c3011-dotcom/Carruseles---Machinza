Mistake 01

Miscalibrated Filling Head

Dosing pistons drift out of tolerance over time, filling above or below the target volume without visible warning.

⚠ Leads to under/over-fill deviations
3 / 7
Mistake 02
🧪

Product Viscosity Shifts

Temperature or formulation changes alter product flow, so a fixed fill setting no longer delivers a consistent dose.

⚠ Causes inconsistent fill weight batch-to-batch
4 / 7
Mistake 03
⚙️

Poor Line Synchronization

Filling, capping and conveying run on independent timing, creating bottlenecks, spills or crushed containers at handoff points.

⚠ Triggers jams and contamination risk
5 / 7
Consequence
Real cost

Batch Rejection

Out-of-spec dosing or contamination risk in sterile products means the entire lot can be scrapped — not just one unit.

Lost raw materials, downtime and delayed release to market.
