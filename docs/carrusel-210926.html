01 / What it is

What is batch validation?

It's the formal process that verifies a produced batch meets every specification: identity, potency, purity, and stability, before it can be released.

Every result is compared against approved limits. If one value falls outside range, the batch cannot move forward.

03 / Why it matters

Nothing ships without confirmation

Quality release is the final checkpoint: a qualified reviewer signs off only when every record and test result confirms compliance.

This discipline protects patients, regulators' trust, and the integrity of every batch that leaves the plant.
