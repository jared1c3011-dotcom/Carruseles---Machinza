What It Is

What Is Batch Validation?

A documented process confirming that a manufactured batch consistently meets its predefined quality specifications, before it is released for distribution.

It links raw materials, process parameters, and lab results into one traceable record.

Proof Required

What Confirms Compliance

  • 01Batch record & process log review
  • 02Lab test results (purity, potency, sterility)
  • 03Equipment qualification & calibration data
Why It Matters

Why No Product Ships Without It

Skipping validation risks patient safety, regulatory penalties, and product recalls.

Every release decision is backed by evidence — not assumption.
